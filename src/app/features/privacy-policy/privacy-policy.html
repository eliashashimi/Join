Privacy Policy

Introduction

Welcome to Join. We take the protection of your personal data very seriously. This privacy policy informs you about how we handle personal data when you use our kanban project management tool developed as part of a student bootcamp project at the Developer Akademie.

Data Collection and Usage

As an educational project, we limit data collection to the absolute minimum required to provide core functionality (such as managing tasks, creating contacts, and organizing boards). The data you enter (e.g., task descriptions, contact names, and email addresses) is stored locally or within our shared test database exclusively for the purpose of demonstrating and testing the application.

Cookies and Local Storage

Our application may use local storage mechanisms (such as browser local storage or session tokens) to maintain your login session and preferences. This data remains on your device and is not shared with third-party tracking services.

Your Rights

Since Join is an educational student project, you have the right to request information about any test data stored by you, or to ask for its deletion at any time by contacting the development team members.
